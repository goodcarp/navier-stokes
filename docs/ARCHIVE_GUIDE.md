# New Forced Route

This guide originated with the first snapshot. See the [pass12 update](pass12-scope-update.md) for the current addendum and its scope.

**What survives ordinary viscosity—and what separates a finite gain from a
Navier–Stokes blowup construction?**

This archive brings together two independent research lines
around the September 2026 forced-fluid results. It follows the argument
from source scrutiny and concrete obstructions to compact initial data,
full-pressure feedback calculations, and a coupled three-dimensional
numerical experiment. Proofs, failed mechanisms, certificates, referee
notes and reproducible code remain inspectable together.

**The ordinary Navier–Stokes blowup problem remains open here.** There are
actual finite local gain lemmas and certified favorable initial
inequalities. There is no validated useful whole-space stage for the
current candidate, no proved inherited return, and no completed singular
assembly. Read the [evidence status and corrections](evidence-status.md)
before quoting a historical “proved,” “PASS,” or “all measured” label.

## The strongest completed pieces

The [expanded cross-review](cross-review-clock-line-expanded.md)
identifies substantial analytic progress behind the unfinished assembly:
a solution-adapted reference that removes an artificial feedback term,
shell-dependent strain stability, a convergent angular-tail estimate,
and weighted estimates for the true field. A newly collected
maximum-principle/plateau-repair draft writes out additional arguments and
detects a consequential receiver-placement error. Its reviewed revision
and remaining issues are explicit; it is not a completed S3 proof.

- **A finite receiving-velocity gain from compact data.** The
  [local pressure and remainder argument](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass3/quantitative-core-stage.md)
  concerns an actual smooth ordinary-NS solution at positive viscosity.
  A [receiving-radius selection](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass3/rotational-receiver.md)
  realizes one-step scale ratios on that same solution. The interval may
  be very small; the exterior field is not regenerated by the lemma.
- **Simultaneous initial feedback in one explicit 3D field.** For
  `u0=M+1020v+(1/4)w_L`, `nu=1/1000`, the
  [fixed-amplitude result](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass9/fixed-amplitude-initialization.md)
  gives positive initial core-ratio growth, core-ratio acceleration,
  receiving mean transfer and fluctuation-energy growth. The pressure
  and viscosity are retained; analytical reductions, exact checks and
  outward interval bounds support the stated finite inequalities.
- **Obstructions that change the search.** The
  [axisymmetric circulation argument](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass6/circulation-return-obstruction.md)
  excludes the specified shrinking return class with persistent
  normalized rotation. The
  [time-polynomial residual test](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass10/time-polynomial-residual.md)
  rules out one proposed validator. These are scoped negative results,
  not global regularity claims.
- **A concrete validation interface.** The
  [endpoint bridge](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass9/evolution-validation-bridge.md),
  [ordered-derivative error hierarchy](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass10/sharp-error-hierarchy.md),
  and [rational slab propagator](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass11/validated-error-propagation.md)
  specify how full-space residual and initial-error bounds would certify
  a retained gain. The comparison algorithm has independent tests;
  its required fluid inputs remain uncertified.
- **Reusable formal and reproducibility components.** The
  [five Lean developments](../lean/README.md) contain 91 elementary
  algebraic, geometric and integral statements with a stored
  standard-axiom report. They do not formalize the surrounding PDE
  theorems. The [historical 94-run reproduction](../strained-core/REPLICATION.md)
  checks its specified pass2–9 snapshot and retains its recorded limits.

## Where the current experiment stops

[Pass11](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass11/REPORT.md) evolves the
complete compact datum's numerical representation, including the mean,
meridional motion, axial endcaps, viscosity and generated angular modes.
It records an uninterrupted finite-cylinder experiment through `T=.001`
and its comparison tests. A periodic axial direction and a radial no-slip
wall remain part of that numerical problem.

![Two time-step runs of the full finite-cylinder diagnostic](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass11/trajectory-summary.png)

*The figure compares numerical trajectories of the same represented datum.
It does not enclose the spatial, boundary or whole-space errors.*

The [diagnostic criteria](../core-line/work/pass11/trajectory-diagnostic-criteria.md)
separate receiving mean, fluctuation energy, central strain/rotation and
full receiving velocity. Small positive margins need spatial, domain,
initial-reconstruction and full-residual error bounds before they become
claims about whole-space NS. Mode energies alone do not determine the
phase-sensitive torque or the next inherited field.

The other branch's
[log-clock assembly](../theorems/03_log_clock/THEOREM_S3/THEOREM_S3.md)
remains conditional. Its own missing assumptions and the additional
consistency issues found in the
[publication scope review](cross-review-clock-line.md) are prominent in
the current guide. The historical theorem title is not a claim that the
assembly is complete or sharp.
