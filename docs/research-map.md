# The argument across both research branches

The starting question is whether the announced smooth-forced Euler
construction offers a path to ordinary Navier–Stokes blowup. The useful
answer is a sequence of narrower statements, not a transfer by changing
one term in the equation.

For the most recent assessment of the clock line, start with
the [expanded source review](cross-review-clock-line-expanded.md). It explains
the positive analytic contributions and the new live closure draft,
alongside the earlier [base-snapshot scope audit](cross-review-clock-line.md).
The [pass12 update](pass12-scope-update.md) distinguishes the later clock
addendum's labels from the gradient passage now repaired under explicit
solution assumptions.

## 1. Read the release at the strength it actually states

The [event record](../analysis/00_event_record_2026-09-08.md) and
[formal-statement audit](../audit/01_lean_statement_audit.md) separate the
paper statements, trusted Lean challenges, emitted proof objects and
independent replay. Smooth-forced Euler, fractional dissipation and
ordinary viscosity are different targets. The secondhand ordinary-NS
claim is not a proof supplied by this repository.

The [two fences](../theorems/01_two_fences.md) state bounded-velocity
continuation and symmetry restrictions with explicit solution/force
hypotheses. The [forced axisymmetric note](../theorems/02_forced_axisymmetric_on_axis_typeII.md)
sharpens the restrictions under its named assumptions. Read the
[scope review](cross-review-clock-line.md#2-necessary-conditions-preserve-the-exact-hypotheses)
for the qualifications on decay, Type I exclusions and swirl-free forcing.

## 2. Test the old architecture before building a new one

The [layered-cascade analysis](../analysis/01_dissipation_ceiling_of_the_layered_cascade.md)
and [core-line viscosity audit](../core-line/work/viscosity-audit.md) expose severe
damping competition in the specified released scale family. The frozen
amplitude model and its exponent arithmetic have narrower scope than a
full PDE nonexistence theorem. This is a reason to change a construction,
not a reason to rule out every positive-dissipation route.

The separate [log-clock branch](../theorems/03_log_clock/THEOREM_S3/THEOREM_S3.md)
investigates a finite vorticity amplification time. Its partial lemmas,
budget evaluations and blind referee notes are valuable source material.
The assembled result remains conditional and has concrete unresolved
consistency issues; [the current review](cross-review-clock-line.md#1-the-log-clock-assembly-is-not-a-proved-sharp-clock)
explains why its favorable numerical column is not a finished theorem.

There is more in that branch than the assembled budget. The
[U1 self-consistent-reference idea](../clock-line/source-campaign/deepest-think/DTC-2026-09-06/s3close/round2/u1/PROOF.md)
couples the reference strain to the actual exterior field, aiming to
remove an artificial feedback cost. Its foundation is the
[far/near strain identity](../clock-line/source-campaign/deepest-think/DTC-2026-09-06/rebuild/far-near-kernel-lemma/NOTE.md).
The [U2 off-plateau argument](../clock-line/source-campaign/deepest-think/DTC-2026-09-06/s3close/round2/u2/PROOF.md)
and [maximum-principle/plateau work](../clock-line/source-campaign/deepest-think/DTC-2026-09-06/s3close/round2/pmax-h3v/)
target named missing estimates. These are substantive analytic directions
to examine on their own hypotheses, not evidence that every numerical
constant or the assembled PDE conclusion is already certified.

## 3. Build an actual compact pressure mechanism

[Passes2–4](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass4/REPORT.md) construct
compact divergence-free cores and calculate the pressure generated by
both the core and its exterior. For the specified compact radial
extension of `diag(−b,−b,2b)+Omega J`, the core's own central pressure is

`p_zz = −(18/7)b² + (2/5)Omega²`.

This is not the pressure of an unlocalized affine field. The
[finite receiver theorem](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass3/rotational-receiver.md)
then uses the actual local NS solution and an explicit remainder bound.
It supplies finite gain, while the [output-profile audit](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass3/terminal-profile-gap.md)
identifies what a scalar gain fails to reproduce.

## 4. Make simultaneous initial signs compatible

[Pass5](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass5/CERTIFIED_INITIAL_FEEDBACK.md)
uses radial reductions and interval bounds to certify a full initial
pressure-feedback inequality. [Pass6](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass6/REPORT.md)
rejects an axisymmetric shrinking return class and introduces a genuinely
3D seed. [Pass7](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass7/REPORT.md) retains
all pressure interactions in the common-family test.

[Pass8](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass8/REPORT.md) distinguishes
a trailing packet's depletion from a new leading-envelope datum. The
leading field has compatible favorable initial core, mean and seed-energy
signs. [Pass9](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass9/fixed-amplitude-initialization.md)
chooses the explicit rational amplitude 1020. It is **off neutral**:
its derivative identities include that nonzero initial core-ratio slope.
The field is a new initial condition, not the regenerated endpoint of
the trailing packet.

## 5. Replace initial jets by a real evolution test

The [affine-envelope model](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass9/affine-envelope-budget.md)
and planar pulse are deliberately scoped model results. They help select
an experiment; they do not supply compact three-dimensional persistence.

[Pass10](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass10/REPORT.md) constructs
the complete initial field and identifies pressure-image and derivative
errors. It also shows why the original uniform Sobolev majorant and a
linear Taylor approximation are poor validators for this datum.

[Pass11](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass11/REPORT.md) adds coupled
full 3D finite-cylinder evolution, calibrated discrete operators, faster
equivalent implementations, and a rational comparison-system propagator.
The experiment and validator remain separate until a smooth whole-space
reconstruction supplies rigorously enclosed coefficients, initial errors
and residuals. The [endpoint criteria](../core-line/work/pass11/trajectory-diagnostic-criteria.md)
say which margins must survive those errors.

[Pass12](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass12/REPORT.md) builds
a compact whole-space potential reconstruction of the inherited endpoint
increment, retaining the exact analytic initial field. It exposes a
sampled least-squares failure and replaces it with a continuous-L2 fit.
This is a C6/H7 endpoint approximation; it still lacks a time-slab
residual and a certified H4 radius. The actual-matrix C4 reference now
has a deformation estimate compatible with such an H4 radius, and the
separate drift–diffusion lemma repairs a gradient-decay/initial-trace
passage on its precise solution class.

## 6. The unsolved step is inherited state, then iteration

A useful future stage must return the **same evolved full field** to a
named stable successor class. Pressure-induced core deformation, packet
geometry, relative phases, generated meridional motion and exterior tails
remain in that state. Isotropic scaling does not reset them.

The [current next target](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/NEXT_TARGET.md)
records this gap. A validated finite episode would be progress; a
singular construction additionally needs an iterated assembly with all
regularity, energy, forcing and terminal conditions proved. Neither
branch has completed that assembly.
