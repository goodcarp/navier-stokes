## What the new core-line pass establishes

The actual central matrix drives a C4 reference whose local deformation
estimate is compatible with an H4 error radius. The gradient note gives
a spatial-center-independent estimate by translating with the drift,
then an initial-trace-uniform weighted bound under an explicit
classical-solution representation premise. These are written conditional
proofs with independent review and selected exact algebra checks. No
actual NS drift/error inputs or useful whole-space stage are certified.

The new endpoint representation adds a compact potential increment to
the exact analytic initial field. Its velocity is C6/H7, divergence-free
and regular on the axis. A sampled least-squares fit failed between
nodes; the corrected continuous-L2 selector removes that observed
failure. Its positive numerical margins remain sensitive to the trial
space and have no enclosed H4 error or full time-slab residual. The four
datasets preserve both failed and corrected fits; none is a new
time-integrated NS solution or a retained-gain certificate.

## How this relates to the clock-line labels

The upstream [S3 addendum](../theorems/03_log_clock/THEOREM_S3/ADDENDUM_1_2026-09-08.md)
labels M1 and M2 “DISCHARGED” and records a 201/201 gate for the
[written pmax/plateau note](../theorems/03_log_clock/pmax_h3v/PROOF.md).
That is substantive new source material. Its labels do not supersede the
hypotheses or convert sampled constants into certified upper bounds.

The pass12 [gradient repair](../core-line/outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass12/comoving-gradient-decay.md)
addresses the center-dependent estimate and, for its smooth
bounded-gradient datum class, the initial-time passage. It distinguishes
classical or represented solutions from an unspecified bounded solution.
The clock datum's nonsmooth angular approximation and global drift
bootstrap still have to meet those exact premises. Spatial Lipschitz
regularity plus time continuity alone does not imply the cited
parabolic-Hölder regularity.

The separate V.4′ covariance issue remains: diagonal affine covariance
and the special variance identity require affine structure, while the
displayed drift hypotheses are more general. A general-covariance
version or an explicit additional hypothesis is needed. Its numerical
plateau budget also uses safety factors applied to sampled suprema;
that procedure does not prove the needed global upper bounds. Pass12
does not certify blanket M2 closure, the displayed “proved” threshold,
a completed S3 theorem, or a refutation of the cited published PDE bound.

The second-host 94/94 reproduction concerns its historical passes2–9
snapshot. It does not cover every finite result in later passes. The
external Euler build capture still lacks a successful final build,
axiom output and comparator replay; source auditing and expected output
are distinct from a completed independent kernel replay.

These qualifications retain the positive components while stating what
they support. Ordinary-NS blowup, a useful validated whole-space stage,
and inherited iteration remain open.
