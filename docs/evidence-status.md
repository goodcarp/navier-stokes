# Evidence status and current corrections

This is an open research archive. The strongest completed claims are finite
mathematical statements and bounded computations with explicit hypotheses.
Neither research branch supplies an ordinary Navier–Stokes singularity, a
validated useful whole-space stage for the current three-dimensional datum,
or an inherited return that can be iterated to blowup.

The [dated pass12 scope update](pass12-scope-update.md) is the current
companion to the newly committed clock addendum. The gradient repair is
conditional on its stated solution/datum class; it does not validate
blanket M2 closure or a sampled plateau budget. The C6/H7 potential
reconstruction is an endpoint diagnostic, with no enclosed NS residual.

## How to read a result

| Label | Meaning here | What it does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| Analytic finite result | A stated finite lemma has a written argument and identified assumptions; audits are linked where available. | A proof assistant has checked every PDE step. |
| Certified finite computation | Exact symbolic/rational checks or outward interval enclosures establish the specified finite calculation. | Every analytic reduction, interval-library implementation, or infinite-dimensional PDE hypothesis has been formalized. |
| Lean core | A small formal theorem has recorded source, pinned dependencies and axiom output. | The surrounding PDE theorem is formalized. |
| Numerical diagnostic | A finite discretization, quadrature or model was evaluated and its recorded checks passed. | Its distance to a whole-space NS solution is rigorously bounded. |
| Conditional route | A conclusion follows only if the named analytic, residual, lifespan, geometric or return conditions can be supplied. | Those conditions have been achieved by this candidate. |
| Obstruction / negative result | A specific construction, norm bound or return class fails under the stated premises. | All routes to NS blowup are excluded. |

`PASS` in an output belongs to that program and its input. It is never a
repository-wide proof verdict. A reproduced result retains its original
scope; reproduction does not promote a model calculation to a PDE theorem.

## Corrections to inherited wording

The [expanded assessment](cross-review-clock-line-expanded.md) is the current
companion to the initial base-snapshot scope review. It recognizes the
useful U1, shell-stability, angular-tail and U2 components, and a newly
appeared written maximum-principle/plateau-repair draft. Consequently
“unwritten” descriptions in the earlier audit refer to its reviewed
historical assembly, not to the absence of this newer draft. The new
draft has a pinned reviewed hash, placeholders and specific open technical
conditions; it does not yet close S3.

The original documents are preserved so their chronology and audits remain
inspectable. The following qualifications take precedence over promotional
or inconsistent labels in those historical files.

1. **The S3 log-clock assembly is conditional and incomplete.** Its own
   [assembled note](../theorems/03_log_clock/THEOREM_S3/THEOREM_S3.md)
   acknowledges missing hypotheses. In particular the exact plateau
   condition is not satisfied by its tanh datum, and the global viscous
   strain estimate is not established. The current independent review
   also identifies interval/range and profile-normalization issues.
   “All proved” budget labels and a finite-grid monotonicity check do not
   repair these issues. Do not quote it as a proved sharp clock, completed
   blowup construction, or refutation of a published PDE result.
2. **The local Lean project has 91 recorded theorem declarations, not the
   33 reported in the old root README.** Its
   [own scope explanation](../lean/README.md) explicitly excludes the PDE
   arguments. The algebra, measure and radial integral cores are useful
   formal components; they are not formal proofs of the associated NS
   or Euler conclusions. No new Lean build is implied by this archival
   publication.
3. **External Euler replay remains separately scoped.** The inherited
   [replication note](../replication/README.md) records a build in progress
   and an unattempted comparator replay. Expected future output is not a
   completed independent kernel replay. The authors' published results,
   our source-scope review, and a locally replayed proof are distinct
   kinds of evidence.
   A [14:13Z read-only capture](external-replication-update.md) locates the
   later log at [8740/9776], but still finds no final build/axiom output.
4. **The historical 94/94 core-line reproduction applies to its preserved
   pass2–9 snapshot.** Read the
   [replication record](../strained-core/REPLICATION.md), including the
   excluded runs and recorded differences. It does not cover later
   passes. Its shorthand about a pressure-error bound should be read with
   the pass9 note's precise distinction between an upper-bound expression
   and a two-sided enclosure of the actual pressure error.
5. **Later full 3D evolution is a finite-cylinder diagnostic.** The
   periodic axial images, radial wall, reconstruction, truncation and
   space-time residual remain obligations. Accurate time refinement,
   energy balance or discrete divergence alone does not validate a
   whole-space stage. The current endpoint must not be reset to the
   original compact profile when testing a successor.

The detailed [clock-line scope review](cross-review-clock-line.md) gives exact

## What remains open

- Ordinary-viscosity NS finite-time blowup in the applicable Clay class.
- A useful full-space evolution enclosure for the selected compact datum.
- A stable successor class containing the actual normalized evolved field,
  including its generated modes, core deformation and exterior tails.
- An iterated assembly with shrinking stages, finite energy, and admissible
  forcing or a genuinely unforced solution.
- Completion of the separate log-clock branch's missing PDE estimates and
  resolution of the inconsistencies recorded above.

The archive records progress toward those targets and the failures that
constrain them. It does not mark the research goal complete.
