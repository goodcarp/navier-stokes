# The actual initial mean jet for a nonconstant chiral envelope

This note derives the exact initial second derivative of the azimuthal
mean angular momentum for the Pass8 leading family. It retains the
envelope derivatives, full pressure, and axial covariance flux.
It is an initial-jet identity, not a frozen-polarization evolution or
a finite-time stage estimate.

The symbol \(r_*\) denotes the exact critical radius of
\(g(r)=r^2\chi(r^2/a^2)\), \(a=63/200\), isolated by
\[
 0.2154755753334<r_*<0.2154755753336,\qquad g'(r_*)=0.
\]
The rounded decimal is not substituted for this exact critical point.

## 1. Datum and pressure convention

The mean near \((r_*,z=\pm4)\) is
\[
 (U_r,U_\theta,U_z)=(cr,AV_0(r),-2cz),\qquad
 c=7/5,\quad V_0(r)=rC_0(r),\quad C_0(r)=\chi(r^2/a^2).
\]
The actual fluctuation there is
\[
 v_r=-\lambda m e(r)\sin\phi/r,\qquad
 v_\theta=-\lambda e'(r)\cos\phi+\lambda k e(r)\sin\phi,\qquad
 v_z=0,\quad \phi=m\theta+kr.
\]
For the leading family, \(m=4,\ k=-20\),
\(e(r)=\chi((r-7/50)^2/(1/10)^2)\),
\(1/8\le\lambda\le1/4\), \(\nu=1/1000\), and \(900<A=A_\lambda<1020\).
The named exact \(A_\lambda\) is the Pass8 central-pressure retuning.
Both axial cutoffs equal one on a neighborhood of each target circle.

Let \(p_m\) be the complex unit mixed-pressure amplitude for the
interaction of the unit outer swirl and this unit seed. The actual
initial pressure component at angular frequency \(m\) is
\(\lambda A\operatorname{Re}(p_m e^{im\theta})\). Its full equation is
\[
 -\Delta_m p_m=s_m,\qquad
 \Delta_m=\partial_{rr}+r^{-1}\partial_r+\partial_{zz}-m^2r^{-2}.
 \tag{1}
\]
Globally, with \(b=e(r)e^{ikr}\) and the unchanged axial product
\(F_z=q_s q_v\),
\[
 s_m(r,z)=\frac2r\left[(V_0b')'
                -\frac{m^2}{r}V_0'b\right]F_z(z).
 \tag{2}
\]
The factor two is already included in this definition.
The affine meridional pump/seed mixed source is zero, even when
\(e'\ne0\): the seed is horizontal and divergence free and its support
is wholly on the pump's affine plateau. Core/seed local products have
disjoint supports. The seed self-pressure has only modes \(0,2m\),
so it pairs to zero with one seed factor in the initial covariance
equations. These are exact initial frequency cancellations.

## 2. Actual covariance derivatives on the axial plateau

Write
\[
 B_\lambda=\frac{\lambda^2mk}{2},\qquad F=e^2,\qquad
 R=\overline{v_rv_\theta},\quad Z=\overline{v_zv_\theta}.
\]
Initially,
\[
 R_0=-B_\lambda F/r,\quad Z_0=0,\qquad
 \mathcal T_0=-r^{-1}\partial_r(r^2R_0)
             =B_\lambda(F'+F/r).
 \tag{3}
\]
All initial third moments vanish by the single angular frequency.
The exact general Reynolds equation from Pass8 gives
\[
 \boxed{\begin{aligned}
 R_t(0)={}&c\mathcal T_0
 +\lambda^2A\left[
    \frac{V_0}{r}(e'^2+k^2e^2)
   -\frac{m^2e^2}{2r^2}(V_0'+V_0/r)\right]\\
 &+\frac{\nu B_\lambda}{r}H_\nu+R_t^p ,
 \end{aligned}}                                                     \tag{4}
\]
where
\[
 H_\nu=-4ee''+2e'^2-\frac{2ee'}r+2k^2e^2
                         +\frac{(2m^2+1)e^2}{r^2}.
 \tag{5}
\]
The pump term is \(c\mathcal T_0\), obtained from
\(-cr\,R_0'-2cR_0\); it is not generally \(-cR_0\).
The viscous term includes both vector-Laplacian angular-basis terms.
For example its scalar-gradient contraction is
\[
 \overline{\nabla_{\rm sc}v_r\cdot\nabla_{\rm sc}v_\theta}
 =\frac{B_\lambda}{r}
   [ee''-2e'^2+2ee'/r-(k^2+m^2/r^2)e^2].
\]

Set \(B=e'-ik e\). The complete pressure contributions are
\[
 \boxed{R_t^p=\frac{\lambda^2A}{2}\operatorname{Re}
       \{e^{-ikr}[B\,p_{m,r}-m^2e\,p_m/r^2]\},}
 \tag{6}
\]
\[
 \boxed{Z_t^p=\frac{\lambda^2A}{2}\operatorname{Re}
                   \{e^{-ikr}B\,p_{m,z}\}.}                         \tag{7}
\]
At the target circles \(Z_t=Z_t^p\): all other initial vertical
covariance contributions vanish on the affine axial plateau.
Neither (7) nor its axial derivative vanishes by symmetry about the
individual packet center.

## 3. Pressure-flux reduction for an arbitrary radial envelope

The exact mean equation is
\[
 G_t+U_rG_r+U_zG_z=\nu\Delta_*G+\mathcal T,\qquad
 \mathcal T=-r^{-1}\partial_r(r^2R)-r\partial_zZ.
\]
Differentiating both pressure fluxes (6)-(7), and then using the
full Poisson equation (1), cancels every undifferentiated \(p_m\) term.
The result is
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal T_t^p=
 \frac{\lambda^2A}{2}\operatorname{Re}
   \{e^{-ikr}[D\,p_{m,r}+rB\,s_m]\},}                               \tag{8}
\]
\[
 \boxed{D=(k^2r+m^2/r)e-r e''-e'+ik(e+2re').}                       \tag{9}
\]
Thus only a radial derivative of the actual whole-space pressure
remains. The cancellation uses the \(p_{zz}\) term generated by the
axial covariance flux; omitting that flux would give a different,
incorrect formula.

On the axial plateau,
\[
 e^{-ikr}s_m=\frac2r\left[
  V_0'(e'+ik e)+V_0(e''+2ik e'-k^2e)-m^2V_0'e/r\right].
\]
Its explicit real contribution in (8) is
\[
 \frac12\operatorname{Re}(e^{-ikr}rB s_m)
 =V_0'(e'^2+k^2e^2-m^2ee'/r)+V_0e'(e''+k^2e).
 \tag{10}
\]

## 4. Exact compact formula at the mean-maximizing circle

Put \(L_0=-cr\partial_r+\nu(\partial_{rr}-r^{-1}\partial_r)\).
At \(r_*\), \(G_0=Ag\) has zero radial derivative and is flat axially.
Therefore the terms involving \(U_t\cdot\nabla G_0\) vanish exactly,
and
\[
 G_{tt}(0,r_*,\pm4)=A L_0^2g+L_0\mathcal T_0+\mathcal T_t(0).
\]
Combining the explicit source term (10) with the local swirl
covariance production gives
\[
 \boxed{\begin{aligned}
 G_{tt}(0,r_*,\pm4)={}&A L_0^2g+\lambda^2A\,\mathcal S\\
 &+B_\lambda[-2c(2F'+rF'')+\nu J_\nu] ,
 \end{aligned}}                                                     \tag{11}
\]
where every function on the right is evaluated at the exact circle,
\[
 \boxed{\begin{aligned}
 \mathcal S={}&
 \frac{m^2e^2}{2r^2}g''
 -C_0\left[e'^2+k^2e^2
           +r e'(e''+k^2e)-\frac{m^2ee'}r\right]\\
 &+\frac12\operatorname{Re}(e^{-ikr}D\,p_{m,r}),
 \end{aligned}}                                                     \tag{12}
\]
\[
 \boxed{\begin{aligned}
 J_\nu={}&6(e'e''+ee''')+\frac{6ee''}r
          -4k^2ee'-\frac{2k^2e^2}r\\
         &-\frac{(4m^2+8)ee'}{r^2}
          +\frac{(2m^2+4)e^2}{r^3},
 \end{aligned}}                                                     \tag{13}
\]
and
\[
 L_0^2g=c^2r^2g''-2c\nu r g'''
              +\nu^2(g''''-2g'''/r+3g''/r^2).
 \tag{14}
\]
The coefficient \(B_\lambda\) is negative for the leading phase.
The pump term in (11) and the \(e'''\) viscous term are therefore
not eligible for omission or an old flat-envelope sign substitution.

As a check, setting \(e=1\) and all its derivatives to zero makes
the pump correction vanish, reduces \(D\) to
\(k^2r+m^2/r+ik\), and reduces (11)-(14) exactly to the previously
audited flat-envelope formula.

## 5. Scope for evaluation and evolution

The only nonlocal input in (11) is \(p_{m,r}\) for the new seed.
The old numerical pressure-gradient bound \(<5\) was certified
for the old trailing profile and cannot be reused unchanged.
The structural harmonic-slab estimate is still valid because
the axial source product is unchanged; its residual norm must be
evaluated with the new radial Green solution.

The same derivative-reduced whole-space trial is available:
\[
 P'=-2C_0 b'
       +m(m-1)r^{-m-1}\int_0^r t^m C_0'b\,dt
       -m(m+1)r^{m-1}\int_r^\infty t^{-m}C_0'b\,dt.
\]
Here \(b'=e^{ikr}(e'+ik e)\); its envelope part must be retained.
The companion diagnostic can bound its error using the new
weighted radial norm and both exact radial tails.

Formula (11) is for the exact initial radial critical circle.
At a neighboring radius with \(g'\ne0\), the actual mean radial
acceleration contributes an additional term
\(-A U_{r,t}(0)g'\), and the unsimplified local terms also contain
\(g'\). This maximum-circle formula must not be used as a grid-wide
evolution equation.

A radial maximizing branch at fixed axial height, when required,
has the separate positive envelope correction
\(-G_{tr}^2/(A g'')\). A global maximum can also choose an axial
location. Neither a fixed-circle jet nor a fixed-height maximizing
jet determines a finite-time interval without a remainder estimate.

The standalone checker verifies the covariance and both forms of
its viscosity, the full mixed source, cancellation using both
pressure fluxes, the maximum-circle source/shear simplification,
all pump and viscous transport terms, and the exact flat limit.
