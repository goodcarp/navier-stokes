## Two useful sources, with their exact limits

**Actual ordinary NS, but topology rather than amplification.** Ciampa–Lucà, *Localization of Beltrami fields: global smooth solutions and vortex reconnection for the Navier–Stokes equations*, JFA 287 (2024), 110610, [primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.01369), Theorem 2, pp. 5 and 22, constructs small smooth finite-energy data on \(\mathbb R^3\), at any prescribed \(\nu,T>0\), whose global smooth solution has topologically inequivalent initial and time-\(T\) vortex lines. Initially the vorticity has no zeros; later it has a hyperbolic zero. The proof controls nonlinear errors around heat evolution of carefully chosen localized fields. Theorem 5.3 gives a positive initial interval without zeros. Theorem 1 separately constructs large critical-norm data through localized Beltrami cancellation and nonlinear smallness. Neither theorem supplies velocity amplification, concentration of circulation into a smaller receiving core, or increasing Reynolds number. The useful technique is quantitative localization and control of the Leray-projected nonlinear remainder; the reconnection conclusion itself is not evidence for a regenerative stage.

**A genuine regenerative cascade for a modified operator.** Tao, *Finite time blowup for an averaged three-dimensional Navier–Stokes equation*, [arXiv:1402.0290v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.0290v3), Theorem 1.5, proves blowup with Schwartz data for an averaged bilinear operator preserving the energy cancellation. Definition 1.3 averages rotations, bounded dilations, and order-zero Fourier multipliers. Theorem 5.3 gives a finite quadratic circuit transferring essentially all input-mode energy to its output after a controlled delay; the infinite construction connects rescaled copies. Section 1.3 proposes realization with the true Euler/NS nonlinearity as a research program. The averaging selects and weights interactions that are unavailable as independent controls in ordinary NS. Thus this is a useful model of the required terminal transfer, not an embedding theorem for the physical equation. A candidate that verifies only quadratic energy cancellation or an isolated favorable interaction has not met its central missing requirement: control of every other interaction generated by the actual operator.
