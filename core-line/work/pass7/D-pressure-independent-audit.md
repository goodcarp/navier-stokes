# Independent mathematical audit of the outer-seed D kernel

2026-09-08. **Accepted.** The identity and inequality in `D-pressure-kernel.md` retain the full pressure acceleration and have the correct signs and contact contribution. This review checks the analytical decomposition, not just the symbolic identities.

1. **Mixed advection.** On the whole seed support, `M=cP`, `P=(x,y,-2z)`, while the radial core is disjoint. Since `w_z=0`, `(w dot grad)P=w`; since `div_h w=0`, `div[(P dot grad)w]=partial_x w_x+partial_y w_y=0`. The resulting compact mixed field is globally solenoidal. Its Leray projection is therefore exactly itself. No pressure term from `B(w,w)` is removed by this observation.

2. **Local tensor.** From `-2Pi(M,(w dot grad)w)`, integration by parts gives `-2 sym grad(QM)` as a quadratic seed kernel. The two mixed accelerations give `-P dot grad Q+2Q` after factoring out `c`. For horizontal indices, commuting derivatives of the scalar Newton kernel and using `partial_i P_k=delta_ik` gives `sym grad(QP)=P dot grad Q+Q`. The sum is precisely `-3P dot grad Q`. Compact support removes boundary terms; the pump and seed both avoid the evaluation point in this portion of the calculation.

3. **Annular pressure sign.** Write `p_w=N*partial_ij(w_iw_j)` and `J_eta=N*div(QS_eta)`, with the actual global joins. Then `2int QS_eta dot grad p_w=-2int div(QS_eta)p_w=-2int J_eta partial_ij(w_iw_j)=-2int Hess J_eta:(w tensor w)`. The two spatial integrations by parts preserve the minus sign. The decaying whole-space adjoint supplies the harmonic plateau terms; they are not arbitrary pressure choices. This argument requires no truncation of the seed pressure's angular harmonics.

4. **Core contact term.** The separated seed pressure is harmonic on a neighborhood of the entire core support. The axisymmetric core functional first selects its angular mean, which is still harmonic. For its regular solid harmonics `H2 R^2 P2+H4 R^4 P4+...`, the known radial calculation gives principal-value contribution `8H2/7-(8/5)Mpsi H4`. The distributional Hessian contact contribution is **+4H2**, because the central bilinear source is `-12H2` and `DzzN` has contact coefficient `-1/3`. Thus the total is `36H2/7-(8/5)Mpsi H4=18 p_w,zz/7-(Mpsi/15)p_w,zzzz`. With `Mpsi=5/8`, `p_w,zz=-int Q:ww`, and `p_w,zzzz=-int partial_zz Q:ww`, the kernel is exactly `-18Q/7+partial_zz Q/24`.

5. **Core swirl cancellation.** For axisymmetric pressure, `tr(grad Wpsi Hess p)=0` pointwise: the antisymmetric part has zero Hessian contraction, and the radial derivative term pairs the azimuthal direction with the meridional Hessian. The nonaxisymmetric pressure has the same zero integral after angular averaging. The other mixed terms vanish by disjoint support. In particular `L(Wpsi;w)=0` exactly for this exterior seed; changing the core rotation sign offers no further adjustment of D.

6. **Pointwise signs.** In the stated cone, both horizontal eigenvalues of Q are positive. The bounds on `q(t), p(t), j(t)`, the negative `a_eta` terms, and `0<b_eta<=5` are used in the correct directions. For example `q<=4` and `j>=jmin>0` imply `-j/(28q)<=-jmin/112`. The resulting horizontal inequalities give `L(M;w)<=-(96/5)Cw` because every value of w is horizontal. No sign of a pointwise pressure source is assumed.

7. **Viscosity.** The seed support avoids the origin, where the tensor has a distributional contact. On this support `Delta Q=0`, so two integrations by parts give `2Pi(w,Delta w)=2int Q_ij partial_l w_i partial_l w_j`. Derivatives remain horizontal, and this term is nonnegative. Its coarse bound uses the true maximum horizontal eigenvalue of Q. The horizontal Hessian/Laplacian integral identity is valid for the compact potential in each horizontal plane and includes the cylindrical angular-basis derivatives. The resulting separated R1, R0, Z1, Z0 factors and the prefactor are consistent.
