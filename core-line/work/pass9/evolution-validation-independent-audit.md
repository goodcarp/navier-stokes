# Independent audit of the conditional evolution bridge

Accepted as a conditional validation framework. Neither route has yet
enclosed an actual evolved field, a useful positive-time interval, or
membership of an inherited endpoint in a successor class.

## Preferred H4 endpoint route

The derivative-sum error estimate
\[
 D^+E_4\le560V_5E_4+270E_4^2+\delta_4
\]
has sufficient constants. There are 35 multiindices; transport
commutators have weight 15 and the nontransport term weight 16.
Including the vector-product factor three gives
\(93\sqrt{35}<560\) and \(45\sqrt{35}<270\).
Splitting at two differentiated factors leaves enough H2 regularity
for the lower factor's pointwise bound, with H5 on the approximation
where the highest nontransport derivative falls.

The endpoint bounds (0b)–(0f) correctly follow from, respectively:
pointwise velocity error, the reverse L2 triangle inequality for the
orthogonal fluctuation projection, the two core-gradient functionals
and positive actual rotation, and the bounded receiving weight.
The core-ratio error allowance \((2+\eta_\beta)\rho_4\) is conservative.
The exact initial datum's mean maximum and fluctuation energy require
their own upper enclosures, as stated.

The normalized H4 endpoint error has the correct factor
\(U^{-1}L^{-3/2}\max(1,L^4)\).
H4 reaches C2 geometry. This route is sufficient only for a successor
class actually stable in that norm; the note explicitly retains that
requirement. It does not replace an H7 successor hypothesis by H4.
Monotone intermediate gains and positive curvature at all times are
correctly treated as optional stronger conditions.

## Optional H7 route

The constants \(765\sqrt{120}<8500\) and
\(381\sqrt{120}<4200\) are sufficient after the full divergence-free
transport cancellation. The displayed Riccati expression is a
supersolution: its excess forcing is
\(\delta(D^{-1}-1)\ge0\).
Initial representation/retuning error, all residual components, and
nonzero inherited error at subsequent time slabs are included.

The torque and instantaneous mean-gain error bounds retain the
radial and axial mean transport and every term of
\(\Delta_*G=r(U_{\theta,rr}+U_{\theta,zz})+
U_{\theta,r}-U_\theta/r\).
The fluctuation-energy bounds use contraction only in L2 and the
full first-gradient norm, where rotational averaging is orthogonal.
No unjustified contraction of the higher derivative-sum norm is needed.

The full off-neutral equations for \(b_1,\Omega_1,b_2,\Omega_2\)
and the quotient formula for \(\beta''\) are correct. H7 controls
the fifth spatial derivatives in the viscous curvature acceleration:
\(N(u)-N(v)\) is controlled in H5, which controls the required third
pointwise derivatives of that difference. The constants 128, 256,
512 and 1024 are consistent with the previously audited whole-space
pressure and product bounds. The pressure contact term is retained.
The initial neutral shortcut is not propagated to positive time.

Evaluating the NS functional at the approximate velocity, rather than
twice differentiating an approximate time curve, correctly avoids
silently discarding time derivatives of the residual.

## Trajectories and inheritance

The flow-error inequalities (14) have the correct coefficients.
The final note now explicitly requires bounds on \(\nabla v\) and
\(\nabla^2v\) along the straight segments connecting each compared
pair of trajectories, or global bounds. This resolves the sole
geometric qualification raised during audit. The revised initial
retuning wording also correctly describes its contribution to a
triangle-inequality upper bound, not a lower bound on total error.

The H7 normalization factor
\(U^{-1}L^{-3/2}\max(1,L^7)\) and its H4 counterpart are correct
for fixed axes and positive isotropic spatial/velocity scales.
Translation, scale and rotation uncertainty must be charged
separately as stated. The comparison retains the entire actual
old and exterior field; fitting a new profile does not replace
the inherited endpoint.

The final forcing paragraph correctly distinguishes validation with
a fixed physical force from declaring the full residual to be that
force. The latter needs its own smoothness and assembly argument.

The existing finite bridge checker was run successfully. It checks
the constant arithmetic, supersolution identity and quotient algebra;
it is not an evolved-field or duration certificate.
