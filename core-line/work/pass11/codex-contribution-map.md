## Five contributions worth foregrounding

1. **An explicit finite receiving-velocity gain for one actual 3D NS solution from compact initial data.** The compact active-core pressure calculation includes the core's own pressure and disjoint outer packets. Standard smooth local existence plus explicit profile-norm remainder estimates yield a strictly positive finite rotational modal/RMS gain, uniform over smaller receiving radii. A scalar radius selection realizes the proposed one-step velocity, Reynolds and weighted-energy scale ratios on that same evolved solution. This is an actual ordinary NS local result at positive viscosity, with zero force, rather than prescribed-strain evolution. The receiver is nonmaterial; the surrounding reservoir and output profile are not regenerated. The interval can be very small, and no practically useful decimal duration is certified. Sources: [pressure lemma](../../outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass2/active-core-pressure.md), [quantitative local theorem](../../outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass3/quantitative-core-stage.md), [receiver and scale-selection proof](../../outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass3/rotational-receiver.md), [material flux distinction](../../outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass3/material-core-transfer.md).

2. **A reproducible compact 3D datum with simultaneous certified favorable initial feedback, mean transfer, and fluctuation-energy growth.** Passes5–9 progress from a radial meridional pump to a genuinely three-dimensional leading seed, retaining the full nonlocal pressure and viscosity in the initial inequalities. The presently selected datum is `u0=M+1020v+(1/4)w_L`, `nu=1/1000`. For this same explicit datum the audited bounds include `beta'(0)>17391/700000`, `beta''(0)>4264878809/700000000`, receiving mean derivative `>20499/200`, and `K'(0)/K(0)>12853/2205`. These are actual-PDE initial statements supported by analytical reductions and outward-rounded/exact interval certificates. Smooth local theory gives some simultaneous positive interval; these initial certificates do not establish a useful-size stage or a successor class. Sources: [radial feedback certificate](../../outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass5/CERTIFIED_INITIAL_FEEDBACK.md), [same-family compatibility](../../outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass7/common-family-feedback.md), [leading-family full pressure](../../outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass8/leading-full-pressure-feedback.md), [selected fixed-amplitude theorem](../../outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass9/fixed-amplitude-initialization.md), [independent fixed-amplitude audit](../../outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass9/fixed-amplitude-independent-audit.md).

3. **Mechanism tests that distinguish finite amplification from repeatable transfer.** There are two scientifically different results here. The axisymmetric circulation maximum principle yields an actual no-return theorem for the specified supercritically shrinking class with uniformly nonzero normalized rotation on a controlled scale; it does not prove general axisymmetric regularity. Separately, an exact linear rotating-affine envelope model proves over 15% energy gain at its named finite time, including viscosity, while giving a finite total positive shear-work budget. That is a reduced-model theorem with an infinite-energy affine base and a stated envelope-energy normalization, not compact nonlinear 3D persistence. Together with the local output-strain defect, these results identify concrete reservoir and profile obligations instead of treating a positive initial jet as regeneration. Sources: [rigid-core output mismatch](../../outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass3/terminal-profile-gap.md), [circulation no-return theorem and finite-force extension](../../outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass6/circulation-return-obstruction.md), [exact affine-envelope gain/exhaustion theorem](../../outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass9/affine-envelope-budget.md). The separate [linear-Taylor obstruction](../../outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass10/time-polynomial-residual.md) concerns one approximation/validator, not actual-flow failure.

4. **A conditional path from a full-field approximation to certified endpoint gains, with a tested rational error propagator.** The H4 bridge charges the complete initial and space-time residual errors to endpoint mean transfer, energy, core cone, local velocity/Reynolds gain and inherited profile. The sharper five-component ordered-derivative hierarchy uses symmetric strain on its diagonal; rigid rotation cancels, while higher derivatives remain explicit triangular couplings. Its pass11 implementation encloses comparison-system propagation with rational arithmetic, finite-path linear kernels, nonlinear slab closure, adaptive subdivision and inherited errors. This is an analytical conditional estimate plus a tested enclosure algorithm. Actual full-space trajectory inputs have not been certified, so no fluid error radius or useful duration has been obtained from it. Sources: [H4/H7 validation bridge](../../outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass9/evolution-validation-bridge.md), [sharp hierarchy](../../outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass10/sharp-error-hierarchy.md), [independent hierarchy derivation audit](../../outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass10/sharp-error-hierarchy-independent-audit.md), [rational propagation proof](validated-error-propagation.md), [propagator](validated_error_hierarchy.py), [independent implementation audit](validated-error-independent-audit.md).

5. **A complete coupled 3D numerical diagnostic with explicit discrete structure, calibration failures, and phase-sensitive endpoint comparison.** The selected field is represented with its full pump and both endcaps. The MAC evolution retains mean feedback, meridional motion, viscosity and generated angular modes; its weighted pressure projection, nonnegative viscosity form and energy-neutral discrete nonlinearity have independent structural tests. The fast implementation agrees with the reference to roundoff on the tested grids. Manufactured tests and whole-space initial quadratures expose pressure-image and axial-resolution errors that energy conservation alone misses. The completed baseline is uninterrupted finite-cylinder evolution of one represented initial field, with no packet or core reset. This is a numerical tool/result, not a whole-space NS certificate; its small positive endpoint branch/core margins have no enclosing spatial/domain error bounds. Sources: [complete initial evaluator](../../outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass10/initial-full-field.md), [image calculation and its limited tail certificate](../../outputs/euler-ns-transfer/pass10/initial-periodic-images.md), [current numerical report](REPORT.md), [MAC audit](full-mac-independent-audit.md), [fast/reference audit](fast-full-mac-independent-audit.md), [checkpoint comparator](compare_mac_endpoints.py), [comparator scope/tests](compare-mac-endpoints-audit.md). ## Claims the unified README must avoid

- **“Proved Navier–Stokes blowup,” “validated whole-space trajectory/stage,” “proved return,” or “closed cascade.”** No compatible infinite construction, terminal singular continuation quantity, or stable successor class has been obtained. Finite actual local gain and favorable initial derivatives remain valid at their stated scope.
- **“The current A=1020 datum is pressure neutral.”** It is off neutral: with `d=beta'(0)`, the relevant identity is `beta''(0)=-(p_zz'(0)+32)/2-10d`. The neutral identity belongs to the earlier retuned families.
- **“The planar/affine pulse proves the compact 3D stage,” or “a new candidate is the old solution's regenerated endpoint.”** Those models and initial-data replacements are separate mathematical objects. Fixed-circle, selected radial-branch and unrestricted global-maximum observables must also remain distinct.
- **“Positive baseline endpoint margins are validated gains.”** The pass11 baseline evolves a no-slip finite radial cylinder with periodic z. Time-step agreement, small discrete divergence, total-energy balance and a small highest retained mode do not bound spatial, image, wall, axis, initial-projection or omitted-mode errors. The fast raw nonlinearity is already axially filtered; its norm is not the complete unresolved-product residual.
- **“All checks are formal proof verification,” or “all reported decimals are interval certificates.”** Symbolic/algebra checks, analytical reviews, rigorous interval integrals, manufactured solver tests and exploratory quadratures have different trust and scope. In particular, the exact omitted-image tail bound does not certify the retained image quadrature, and the rational comparison propagator has manufactured inputs rather than certified fluid slabs.
- **“The obstruction rules out all axisymmetric or forced NS constructions.”** Its circulation/profile and finite torque-budget hypotheses matter. Smooth forcing remains allowed by the intended Clay alternatives; it is not artificially forbidden. Existing unforced examples are a choice of datum, not a general restriction on the research route.
