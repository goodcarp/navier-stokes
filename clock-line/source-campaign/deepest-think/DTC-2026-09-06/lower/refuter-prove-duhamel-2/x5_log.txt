Consequence: L2's 'every material element contributes with the same sign, for all time'
and L4's 'P1 >= 0' are properties of the ORIGIN ONLY.  At the material innermost shell the
kernel is sign-INDEFINITE, so the analogue of P1 has no sign, and the reduction of (H2) to
one inequality on one integral does not transfer.  GAP 2 is not the O(1/L) size step the
NOTE describes; it is the loss of the entire sign structure the argument is built on.

What DOES transfer (confirmed): the SIZE.  For the sharp shell the origin value is
a(0) = (M/2) log(R/rho0) EXACTLY (x4b), and the frame's material-shell value is
(M/2) log(R/rho0) + 0.216773 M, so the difference is exactly the constant 0.216773 M.
