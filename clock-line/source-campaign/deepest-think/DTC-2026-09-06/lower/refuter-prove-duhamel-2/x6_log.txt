Reading.  At the origin the negative part is 0 by the proved sign law: a is a POSITIVE
functional, no cancellation.  At the seat's own tracer seed (phi = 45 deg) the negative
part is a finite fraction of a, so the same functional is a DIFFERENCE of two comparable
pieces there.  L2's 'every material element contributes with the same sign, for all time'
and L4's 'P1 >= 0, so only P2 is sign-indefinite' are therefore not available at any point
that carries vorticity.  GAP 2 is a structural gap, not the O(1/L) size step the NOTE calls it.
