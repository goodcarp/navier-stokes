                                                   time T∗ and that T∗ is the first such time, we establish lower bounds on the rate of blow-up of theR t maximum

                                                In previous literature, several variants of the BKM criterion have been shown to hold. Indeed, Fer-

                                           maximum norm of the deformation tensor. Then, Constantin, Fefferman, and Majda [CFM] established a
                                           blow-up criterion involving the vorticity direction and the maximum norm of the velocity. In a result which
                                           directly improves the BKM criterion, Kozono and Taniuchi [KT] demonstrated that the L∞ norm in (1.3)

     These results are, of course, a priori in nature; whether or not solutions to (1.1) with smooth initial
data blow up in finite time remains an open problem. However, under assumptions with initial data in
lower regularity spaces, there have been major advances in recent years. Elgindi [E] established finite-time
singularity formation in the axisymmetric no-swirl class for initial data in C 1,α for sufficiently small α > 0.
We also note the work by Córdoba, Martinez-Zoroa, and Yang [CMZ] in which they established finite-time
blow-up for a class of initial data in C ∞ (R3 \ {0}) ∩ C 1,α ∩ L2 , with α > 0 sufficiently small. Recently,
Shkoller [S] obtained a threshold result by showing finite-time Type-I blow-up in the axisymmetric no-
swirl class with initial data in C 1,1/3− ∩ L2 , constructing solutions satisfying ∥ω(t)∥L∞ ∼ (T∗ − t)−1
as t → T∗− . Finally, we note the paper by Jeong, Martı́nez-Zoroa, and Ożański [JMO], in which they
proved instantaneous and continuous-in-time loss of supercritical Sobolev regularity for (1.1).
     Assuming a solution blows up at a time T∗ , a natural question concerns the rate at which the singularity
forms. Under the assumption of blow-up, one can prove a lower bound on the blow-up rate if the quantity
satisfies, say, a Riccati-type inequality of the form, ẋ ≲ xβ , where β > 1. For a solution u to the 3D
incompressible Euler equations which leaves the class (1.2) at a time T∗ , Chen and Pavlović [CP] proved
pointwise lower bounds on u in the H r norm for t sufficiently close to T∗ of the order (T∗ − t)−2δ/5−1 .
     Recently, there has been interest in the rate of blow-up of the vorticity itself. Motivated by solutions with
self-similar blow-up, Constantin, Ignatova, and Vicol [CIV] established a criterion for the rate of blow-up
of ∥Dω∥L∞ . Assuming ω0 ∈ C 1 and that a solution to (1.1) with finite initial kinetic energy blows up at a
time T∗ , they proved
                                      sup (T∗ − t)1+γ ∥Dω∥L∞ (R3 ) = ∞                                       (1.5)
                                    t∈[0,T∗ )

for all γ < 2/5.
     In this paper, we improve on the classical
                                              Rt      BKM criterion with explicit lower bounds on the blow
up rate of the accumulation of vorticity, 0 ∥ω(s)∥L∞ , and non-integrable lower bounds on the quantity,
sups≤t ∥ω(s)∥L∞ , for t sufficiently close to T∗ . Moreover, we obtain the lower bound (1.5) with γ = 2/5.
Also, for any k ∈ N, we provide pointwise lower bounds on the quantity ∥Dk ω(t)∥L∞ . Finally, we detail
some consequences on the blow-up rate of the deformation tensor. These results are detailed in Section 2.
     In Section 3, we prove Theorem 2.1, which provides lower bounds on ω. To establish the estimate on
the accumulation of vorticity, we first derive a Gronwall-type inequality of the form (3.6) for ∥ω(t)∥L∞ on
the interval [0, T∗ ). However, it turns out that the differential inequality contains an exponential of the accu-
          Rt
mulation 0 ∥ω∥L∞ , which does not seem amenable to a barrier argument. To bypass this, we parameterize
time in terms of the accumulation of the vorticity, called τ , see (3.7) below. We then reformulate our expo-
nential inequality for (d/dt)∥ω(t)∥L∞ in terms of the new parameter. The lower bound on the accumulation
is then obtained after considering dt/dτ .
     In Section 4, we prove Theorems 2.5, 2.6, and 2.7. Theorem 2.5 provides pointwise-in-time lower
bounds on the blow-up of ∥Dω∥L∞ . The proof starts with a lemma stating an interpolation inequality
relating ∥D1/2 ω∥L8 and ∥Dω∥L∞ . We then derive a Riccati-type inequality for the L8 norm, from which we
deduce a lower bound on ∥Dω∥L∞ . We note that this lower bound is at the critical exponent γ = 2/5. This
result implies that the singularity for ∥Dω∥L∞ develops as a pointwise limit to infinity. In Theorem 2.6, we
demonstrate that Theorem 2.5 may be extended to analogous pointwise-in-time lower bounds for ∥Dk ω∥L∞ .
Finally, in Theorem 2.7, we provide lower bounds on the uniform norm of the derivative of the stress
σ = ∇u + (∇u)T considered by Ponce in [P].
