(1) f is strictly decreasing => (b) <=> M >= M_fix
==============================================================================
  d/dM [ c1/(s(1+log(K M^(1/5)))) ] = -c1/(5*M*s*(log(K) + log(M)/5 + 1)**2)
  sign: numerator -c1 < 0, denominator 5 s M (1+log(K M^(1/5)))^2 > 0 on the active branch
  => f strictly decreasing; M - f(M) strictly increasing; the solution set of M>=f(M)
     is exactly [M_fix, inf).  So M_fix is the SHARP explicit form of (b).
