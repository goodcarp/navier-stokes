========================================================================
(1) viscosity power in the Leray/Giga lower bound
========================================================================
  u solves NS with viscosity nu on [0,T).  Put w(x,sigma) = u(x,sigma/nu)/nu.
  Then w solves the nu=1 equations and blows up at Tw = nu*T.
  nu=1 statement (Leray 1934 / Giga 1986):  ||w(sigma)||_q >= c (Tw-sigma)^(-(1-3/q)/2)
  => ||u(t)||_q >= c * nu * (nu (T-t))^(-(1-3/q)/2) = c * nu*(sqrt(nu)*sqrt(s))**((3 - q)/q)
     q=oo:  nu exponent = 1/2,  (T-t) exponent = -1/2
     q=4:  nu exponent = 7/8,  (T-t) exponent = -1/8
     q=6:  nu exponent = 3/4,  (T-t) exponent = -1/4
     q=9:  nu exponent = 2/3,  (T-t) exponent = -1/3
  q=inf:  ||u(t)||_inf >= c nu^(1/2) (T-t)^(-1/2).
