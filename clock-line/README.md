# The clock line: source campaign

The original curated clock-line notes remain in the repository's
[`theorems/`](../theorems/), [`analysis/`](../analysis/),
[`audit/`](../audit/) and [`lean/`](../lean/) trees. They are preserved
without changing their substantive text.

This directory adds the source campaign material that fed those notes:

- [Release analysis](source-campaign/external/alpoge-buckmaster-2026-09-08/):
  the forcing, criticality, symmetry and statement-scope investigation.
- [Log-clock assembly and referee work](source-campaign/deepest-think/DTC-2026-09-06/s3close/README.md).
- Its explicitly named inputs:
  [lower synthesis](source-campaign/deepest-think/DTC-2026-09-06/lower/SYNTHESIS.md),
  [written lemmas and refutations](source-campaign/deepest-think/DTC-2026-09-06/write/),
  and [gap investigations](source-campaign/deepest-think/DTC-2026-09-06/gaps/).
- The explicitly cited
  [far/near exterior-strain kernel](source-campaign/deepest-think/DTC-2026-09-06/rebuild/far-near-kernel-lemma/NOTE.md)
  and [its referee material](source-campaign/deepest-think/DTC-2026-09-06/rebuild/refuter-far-near-kernel/),
  plus the named `sharp/`, `bfg/`, `verify-astra/` and `toys/` dependency
  subtrees required to inspect the earlier arguments and numerical tests.

The source archive includes material beyond the original curated S3 note:
the [self-consistent-reference proposal](source-campaign/deepest-think/DTC-2026-09-06/s3close/round2/u1/PROOF.md),
the [off-plateau estimate](source-campaign/deepest-think/DTC-2026-09-06/s3close/round2/u2/PROOF.md),
and the [maximum-principle/plateau repair work](source-campaign/deepest-think/DTC-2026-09-06/s3close/round2/pmax-h3v/).
Their value and remaining proof obligations should be assessed separately
from the assembled note's unchecked budget labels.

These are **historical working sources**, including stopped runs, partial
referee notes, exploratory code, unsuccessful variants and prior wording.
Collection does not endorse every claim. Read the current
[evidence status](../docs/evidence-status.md),
[expanded assessment](../docs/cross-review-clock-line-expanded.md) and
[initial scope review](../docs/cross-review-clock-line.md) before quoting
the S3 assembly.

Distinct source variants are kept. Their source-relative paths and SHA-256
hashes appear in [the collection manifest](../manifests/collected-sources.json).
No unrelated archive was copied.
